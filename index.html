About

I grew up in Monclova, Ohio, just outside Toledo. My family is still there, and northwest Ohio is still home.

These days I work in Washington as a Legislative Assistant to Congressman Bob Latta of Ohio's Fifth District, covering artificial intelligence, robotics, manufacturing, and commerce policy. Before that I was an AI policy fellow with the House Energy and Commerce Committee.

I work on technology policy because the next decade of it lands squarely on places like home: whether Ohio's data center boom raises or lowers electric bills, whether AI strengthens our factories and farms or hollows them out, and whether kids grow up safe with these tools. Northwest Ohio deserves people at that table who are from there.

Roots

St. John's Jesuit in Toledo, where I rowed crew. Washington and Lee University, where I studied economics and spent four years in the Bonner Program doing community service. Summers at the county fair, winters at Walleye games, and as many Mud Hens home stands as I can make.
